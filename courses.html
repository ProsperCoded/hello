Current Offered Courses

  • CSC 301 - Computer Center Management: Overview of managing modern computing facilities, hardware allocation, software deployment, security policies, and administrative operations.
  • CSC 335 - Software Engineering: Study of software development lifecycles, Agile practices, software architecture, version control systems (Git), testing, and design patterns.
  • CSC 313 - Computer Architecture & Organization: Fundamentals of computer hardware structures, CPU design, instruction set architecture, memory hierarchy, and I/O organization.
  • MAT 312 - Probability Distribution & Elementary Limit Theorem: Mathematical probability models, random variables, expectation, variance, continuous/discrete distributions, and limit theorems.
  • GES 103 - Government, Society and the Economy: Exploration of governmental systems, socio-economic structures, citizenship, governance, and public institutions in Nigeria.
  • GES 301 - Introduction to Entrepreneurship Skills: Principles of venture creation, business planning, opportunity identification, financial management, and innovation.
